Hello There! Im currently a masters student at NYU. In my spare time I'm a researcher at CILVR lab NYU where I study how machines can learn compact representations of the world and then use them to plan. My research interests are in computer vision and representation learning.

Prior to this, I was a student at NITK Surathkal, India where I graduated with a Bachelor of Technology in Artificial Intelligence. My undergraduate thesis was on self-supervised pretraining for geospatial foundation models (advised by Dr. Shrutilipi Bhattacharjee).

I also have 12K trophies on Clash Royale and a super cool minecraft base (proof at the bottom of the site).
